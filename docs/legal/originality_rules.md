# Originality and Asset-Provenance Rules

This file is a production guardrail, not legal advice.

## Identity
Fort Knocks is an original post-collapse portrait artillery game. Inspiration may come from the broader artillery/physics genre, but the product must be independently identifiable through its world, characters, progression, UX, art, audio, levels, writing, and implementation.

## Do not copy
Do not copy or recreate another game's:
- source code,
- characters or character designs,
- story/dialogue,
- named factions or locations,
- distinctive level layouts,
- artwork, sprites, icons, UI, logos, typography treatments,
- music or sound effects,
- marketing copy,
- proprietary data/assets.

Do not use another game's production artwork as an image-to-image or 3D-generation source for Fort Knocks assets.

## References
Reference material should be used to discuss abstract qualities such as readability, pacing, camera language, or genre conventions. New work should be designed from Fort Knocks' own brief and art bible.

## Asset provenance
When production assets begin, track:
- asset ID/name,
- author/source,
- generation/editing tool,
- source prompt/reference owned or licensed for use,
- licence/usage notes,
- date,
- final project path.

## Naming
Working names are not assumed legally cleared. Before public commercial launch, perform appropriate trademark/storefront/domain clearance for the final game title and major branded terms.
